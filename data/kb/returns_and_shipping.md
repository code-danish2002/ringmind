# Shipping and Returns

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free and takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs $14.99 and takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM ship the same business day. Tracking is sent by text and email once the order ships.

## Returns
Devices and accessories can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are in like-new condition with all original accessories. Opened devices are subject to a $35 restocking fee unless they are defective. Start a return online or at any store; we email a prepaid shipping label.

## Damaged or lost deliveries
If a package arrives damaged or is marked delivered but not received, report it within 7 days. We will open an investigation and usually ship a replacement within 2 business days.

# Billing and Payments

Bills are issued monthly on the customer's billing date. Payment is due 21 days after the bill is issued.

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer (ACH), and payment at any of our retail stores. Customers can enable AutoPay in the app or online account and receive a $5 per month discount.

## Late payments
If a payment is more than 10 days late, a $10 late fee is added. Service may be suspended if a balance is more than 30 days overdue. Suspended service is restored within 2 hours of payment.

## Disputes and refunds
Customers can dispute a charge within 60 days of the bill date. A billing specialist reviews disputes within 5 business days. Approved refunds are credited to the next bill or back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.

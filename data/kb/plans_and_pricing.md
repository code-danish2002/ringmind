# Plans and Pricing

## Basic Plan
The Basic plan costs $29.99 per month. It includes 10 GB of mobile data, unlimited calls and texts within the country, and standard-definition video streaming. There is no annual contract.

## Plus Plan
The Plus plan costs $49.99 per month. It includes 50 GB of high-speed data, unlimited calls and texts, 10 GB of hotspot data, and HD video streaming. International texting to 100 countries is included.

## Unlimited Plan
The Unlimited plan costs $69.99 per month. It includes unlimited high-speed data (speeds may be reduced after 100 GB during congestion), 30 GB of hotspot data, 4K streaming, and calling to Canada and Mexico.

## Changing plans
Customers can upgrade at any time and the new plan applies immediately, with the difference prorated on the next bill. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

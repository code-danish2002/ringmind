# Stores and Appointments

## Store locations
Nimbus Downtown is at 120 Main Street. It is open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM, and closed on Sundays.
Nimbus Westfield Mall is on level 2 of Westfield Mall. It is open every day, 10 AM to 9 PM.
Both stores have free parking for customers for the first two hours.

## What you can do in store
Stores handle new phone setup and data transfer, screen and battery repairs, device trade-in appraisals, SIM and eSIM changes, and plan changes. Most repairs are done the same day if the part is in stock.

## Appointments
Appointments are 30 minutes long and can be booked by phone, in the app, or online. Walk-ins are welcome, but customers with an appointment are seen first. Please arrive 5 minutes early and bring a photo ID. Appointments can be cancelled or changed at any time at no charge.

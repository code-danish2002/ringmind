# Support and Troubleshooting

## Support hours
Phone support with a live agent is available Monday to Friday 8 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM, Eastern time. The automated assistant is available 24/7.

## No signal or no service
1. Restart the phone.
2. Turn airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
3. Check that the SIM card is seated properly, or that the eSIM is enabled in settings.
4. Check the network status page for outages in your area.
If none of these work, a ticket should be opened so a technician can check the line.

## Slow data
Check whether the monthly high-speed data allowance has been used up. Toggle mobile data off and on. Reset network settings if the issue continues. Speeds on the Unlimited plan may be reduced after 100 GB during congestion.

## Porting a number
To transfer a number from another carrier, the customer needs their account number and port-out PIN from the old carrier. Porting usually completes within 24 hours.

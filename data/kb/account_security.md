# Account Security

## Account PIN
Every account has a 4-digit account PIN that is set when the account is opened. The PIN is used to verify the account holder on calls and in stores. Customers can change their PIN in the app under Settings, then Security. If a customer has forgotten their PIN, they can verify with their date of birth instead, or reset the PIN in the app.

## What needs verification
Billing details, payment information, cancelling orders, and booking or changing store appointments all require identity verification. Order tracking and general questions do not.

## Protecting your account
Nimbus Mobile will never ask for your full card number, your online password, or a one-time code sent to your phone. If you receive a suspicious call claiming to be from Nimbus Mobile, hang up and call us back on the number on your bill.
